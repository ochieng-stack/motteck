PROUDLY BUILT IN KENYA

Connecting People With What They Need.

MOTTECK is a Kenyan marketplace and connection platform designed to make it easier for people to find vehicles, spare parts, automotive products, services, and the businesses that provide them.

Instead of spending hours searching from one business to another, customers can come to MOTTECK, tell us what they are looking for, discover available options, and connect with the right seller or business.

ABOUT MOTTECK

What is MOTTECK?

MOTTECK is a Kenyan digital marketplace built to bring customers and businesses closer together.

Our platform brings together vehicle listings, automotive products, spare parts, services, businesses, and useful automotive information in one place.

But MOTTECK is about more than simply displaying listings. Our goal is to help people actually find what they are looking for.

If a customer is looking for a particular car, truck, motorcycle, spare part, service, or another automotive solution, they can approach MOTTECK and tell us what they need. We help identify relevant options and connect the customer with the appropriate business or seller.

At the same time, MOTTECK gives businesses a platform where they can showcase what they offer and reach customers who are actively looking for their products and services.
